### 🕌 About The Project

**Syifaudz Dzikri (شفاء الذكر)** is a simple, modern, and user-friendly web-based Qur'an and daily prayer application. Designed to make reading the Holy Qur'an, listening to recitations, searching specific verses, and accessing daily Islamic prayers effortless across all devices — **without requiring an account or login**.

---

### ✨ Key Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>📖 Complete Holy Qur'an (114 Surahs)</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>No account or login required</li>
        <li>Complete <b>114 Surahs</b> (Al-Fatihah to An-Nas)</li>
        <li>Surah number, Arabic & English names, translation & verse count</li>
        <li>Arabic script, Latin transliteration & translation</li>
        <li>Jump directly to any specific Surah or verse</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>🎧 Audio Recitation & 🤲 Daily Prayers</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Play, pause & resume Qur'an recitations smoothly</li>
        <li>Navigate seamlessly between verses while listening</li>
        <li>Follow Arabic text & translation alongside audio</li>
        <li>Curated collection of essential <b>Daily Prayers</b></li>
        <li>Clean, comfortable interface for reading & memorizing</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
